## Forks are welcome

Fork the code, change it, ship it. If you distribute your fork as a built app, a disk image, an App Store listing, a Homebrew cask or anything like them:

- Don't call it Noctule or Noctule Scan, and don't use a name that could be mistaken for either.
- Don't use the Noctule mark, the app icons or any other brand art from the project.
- Do say what it is built on. "Based on Noctule Scan" and "a fork of Noctule Scan" are fine.

## Code identifiers

Identifiers in source code that contain "noctule" may stay in your fork's source: the module names (`NoctuleKit` and the rest), the `noctule` command, the `NOCTULE_*` environment variables and `app.noctule.*` in the source. A fork that ships should still change its bundle identifier and its display name. The repository doesn't make these configurable.
